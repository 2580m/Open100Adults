How to play

How it works

  1. 01

    Pick a story

    Check the partner, intensity and tags, and pick one that fits your mood. You can change what they call you and what you call them.

  2. 02

    Follow the big text

    The big text tells you what to do with your body. The small text is what your partner says. While the beat is playing, move in time with it.

  3. 03

    Close? Hit “I'm close”

    Tapping it starts a hands-off break right away. Your edges are counted, and in some stories they change the ending.

  4. 04

    Choose how hard it is

    Before every story you can pick “Quick” (half the steps, always allowed at the end), “As written” or “Long tease” (each step twice).

  5. 05

    The ending depends on the story

    In stories where your partner counts down from 10 or 5, hitting 0 is your permission to cum. Other stories decide by how many times you edged, or let you choose. If you finish before you're allowed, tap “I came too soon”.

FAQ

FAQ

Is there a voice?
Not yet, it's text only. The only sound is a small beat that sets the pace, with no voice reading. Earphones let you play without worrying about who's around.
How do I stop partway through?
Tap “Quit” in the top right. You can also pause.
Is there anything painful or risky?
There are no instructions to slap, pinch or hurt yourself with toys. If you feel unwell or anything hurts, stop right away.
Where is my progress saved?
How many times you've played and the endings you've seen are kept only in this device's browser. Nothing is sent or collected.
Are there stories for women?
Yes. They're grouped under “For women” in the list, and more are on the way.
