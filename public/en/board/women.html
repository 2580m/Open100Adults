How to play

How to play

  1. 01

    Hit Start and play

    Body, tone and length are already set. Change them whenever you like. Your settings stay on this device.

  2. 02

    Roll, then do what the square says

    Climb from square 1 to the top, row by row. You can see the whole board before you roll, including where each number from 1 to 6 would take you.

  3. 03

    Ladders, snakes and Back to Start

    Land at the foot of a ladder and take its challenge to climb it. Land on a snake's head, do a short command, and slide down to its tail. Cards and slide-backs that drop you on a snake or a ladder count too. The big Back to Start snake lets you stay only if you hold one edge.

  4. 04

    We count the seconds for you

    If a command has seconds in it, a timer pops up and chimes when it's done. No counting in your head.

  5. 05

    Land exactly on the Finish

    Roll too high and you bounce back from the Finish. After 3 bounces, any roll gets you there. Can't do a command or break a Rule, and you slide back 2 spaces (3 on a Tough square), and it counts as a fail. Fewer fails at the Finish means a better verdict.

FAQ

FAQ

Do I need to sign up or pay?
Neither. Open the page and play. It's all free.
Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Your settings, achievements, fewest fails and any game in progress stay in this device's browser. There's no leaderboard and nothing is uploaded.
What if I close the page mid-game?
Your progress saves automatically. Next time, tap “Continue” to pick up on the same square.
Can I play with nothing on hand?
Yes. By default you only get commands that need nothing but your hands. If you have a mirror, ice, lube, a toy, or a scarf or towel, pick them in settings to add commands that use them.
What if I don't want to do a command?
You get 3 swaps per game, and swapping doesn't count as a fail. You can also hit “Can't”; the button tells you how far you'll slide back. If you feel unwell, pause or stop. You can quit any time.
What does “Get out of a snake free” do?
It's one of the Cards. Keep it, and the next time you land on a snake (or the big Back to Start snake) you can use it to stay put instead of sliding down.
What happens after “Denied tonight”?
Play the next day and the verdict ignores 1 of your fails. It grows by one for each day in a row you were denied, up to 3. Skip a day and it resets.
What's today's board?
One layout picked for each day, so everyone climbs the same board that day. Finish it every day to build a streak. Switch to Random if you want a different layout.
