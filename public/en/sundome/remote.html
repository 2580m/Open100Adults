Edging game

Remote play

Someone else gives the commands with buttons, and you hold out exactly as your screen says. Just send a link. No sign-up.

How it works

  1. 1Make a linkThe person holding out makes a link on this page.
  2. 2Send itSend it to one person, or get listed on the site and wait for someone.
  3. 3Follow their buttonsEvery command they press shows up on your screen as is.

What they can do

Commands
Stop, slow, fast and more, in 6 speeds
Lines
Typed messages with someone you know. Preset lines when it's open
Final call
They decide whether you may come or are denied
Extend
They can add more time

Play safe

  • No voice or video is exchanged
  • Your name and contacts are never shown
  • Nothing you press or say is stored
  • Either of you can stop at any time
