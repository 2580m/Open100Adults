How to measure

How to measure

  1. 01

    Length: from the pubic bone on top to the tip

    Press a ruler lightly against the pubic bone on the upper side and measure straight to the tip. Angling the ruler or pressing hard into fat inflates the number.

  2. 02

    Girth: circumference at the thickest point

    Wrap a tape or string around the thickest part and measure it. If you only know the diameter, switch to “Diameter” (we multiply by 3.14).

  3. 03

    Erect means fully hard

    A half-erection reads small. For flaccid, measure at room temperature, not cold. Measuring a few times and using the middle value is more stable.

03
Data

The data and how to read it

  • The baseline: 15,521 men measured by clinicians

    We use the nomograms built by Veale et al. (2015, BJU International) from studies where health professionals measured with a standard procedure. Erect length averages 13.12 cm (SD 1.66), erect girth 11.66 cm (1.10), flaccid length 9.16 cm (1.57), flaccid girth 9.31 cm (0.90).

  • Self-reported surveys run large

    Online surveys and retailer data put the average around 13.5–14 cm, roughly 1 cm higher. Measuring methods vary, and smaller men are less likely to answer. We only use measured data, so this may feel stricter than averages you have seen elsewhere.

  • No country-specific baseline

    There is no large public dataset of clinician-measured men for a single country. Measured studies find only very small differences between populations, so the worldwide measured data is the baseline.

  • How your position is worked out

    Your length and girth are each placed on the normal distribution of the clinician-measured data, then combined into one position. That gives your top or bottom %, your rank and 1-in-N.

FAQ

FAQ

What is the average penis size?
In the clinician-measured data this checker uses (Veale et al. 2015, BJU International, 15,521 men), the average erect length is 13.12 cm (5.17 in) and the average erect girth is 11.66 cm (4.59 in). Flaccid, it is 9.16 cm long and 9.31 cm around.
Erect or flaccid?
Erect is the reference. Flaccid size varies a lot with temperature and nerves, so treat it as a rough guide.
I only know the diameter
Switch to “Diameter” and we convert it to circumference (× 3.14).
Is my result stored?
No. Everything is calculated on your device and nothing is sent to our server. The share link contains your numbers, so choose who you send it to.
My rank is low. Is something wrong?
No. The result only shows where you sit in the distribution. It says nothing about health or how good your sex life is. A urologist can answer any concerns.
The average is different from other sites
Self-reported surveys run about 1 cm large. We only use clinician-measured data.

This shows a statistical position only and is not a medical assessment. Size and sexual satisfaction are different things. For adults 18+.
