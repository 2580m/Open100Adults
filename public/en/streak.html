What people say happens, day by day

This is a summary of what people commonly report. It doesn't promise any medical effect, and the research is still thin.

  1. 1

    First Step

    The most common reaction: “nothing much happened.” The point is to log the day you decided.

  2. 3

    Past Three Days

    The day most often described as the hardest. The peak of urges tends to land here.

  3. 7

    The Week Wall

    Many report their skin or sleep “feels different.” A study claiming testosterone rises on day 7 is often quoted, but it was retracted in 2021 (see “What research has actually shown” below).

  4. 14

    Second Week

    Weeks two and three often bring a flat, unmotivated stretch. A lot of slips happen here.

  5. 21

    Habit Sprout

    Habits start swapping. Many say urges come less often.

  6. 30

  7. 60

    Urges become something you can let pass, many say.

  8. 90

03
Research

What research has actually shown, day by day

Separate from the anecdotes above, this lists only what papers and official manuals have confirmed, in order of days. If you use the counter, your current day is marked.

Start the counter and your current day will be marked here

  1. Days 2–7

    The abstinence window before a semen test

    The WHO semen analysis manual (6th edition, 2021) asks for 2–7 days of abstinence before a test. Semen volume and sperm count change with the number of days, so tests keep to this window.

    WHO laboratory manual for the examination and processing of human semen, 6th edition (2021)

  2. Day 7

    The “testosterone peaks on day 7” study was retracted

    A 2003 study of 28 men reporting that blood testosterone rose to 145.7% on day 7 is widely quoted, but the journal retracted the paper in 2021. There is currently no solid basis for a day-7 testosterone spike.

    Jiang M, Xin J, Zou Q, Shen JW. J Zhejiang Univ Sci 2003; 4(2): 236-240 (retracted 2021)

  3. Days 11–14

    Sperm motility and shape start to decline

    A study of 9,489 semen samples found that even in normal samples, motility and the share of normally shaped sperm were lower after 11–14 days of abstinence. The authors advise sperm donors not to go beyond 10 days.

    Levitas E, et al. Fertil Steril 2005; 83(6): 1680-1686

  4. Day 21

    A small study saw higher testosterone after 3 weeks

    In 10 healthy men, blood testosterone was higher after three weeks of abstinence than before. It's a 10-person study and it didn't measure mood, focus or other felt changes.

    Exton MS, et al. World J Urol 2001; 19(5): 377-382

  5. Day 30+

    No study found on 30- or 90-day effects

    30 and 90 days are popular milestones, but in our search in October 2026 we found no paper testing physical or mental changes at those lengths. Treat the stories as stories.

Tips

How to keep going

  • Decide your bedtime routine

    “Before bed” is the most common slip trigger. Decide where the phone goes before you lie down.

  • Learn your triggers from tags

    A slip is just one tag. The stats show the breakdown, so you learn your weak spots.

  • Watch total, not just streak

    A broken streak doesn't erase your total or best. Logging and continuing beats starting over.

  • Urges crest and pass

    Strong urges usually fade within minutes, people say. Use the red button's 90 seconds to get over the crest.

FAQ

FAQ

Do I need a NoFap app to track my streak?
No. This NoFap tracker runs in your browser with no account. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app. Your data stays on your device, and you can export it as JSON.
Does testosterone really peak on day 7 of NoFap?
The 2003 study behind that claim (28 men, a rise to 145.7% on day 7) was retracted by the journal in 2021. A 10-person study did find higher testosterone after three weeks of abstinence, but there is currently no solid evidence for a day-7 peak.
Does a wet dream reset the streak?
No. This counter only counts actions you chose. You don't need to log wet dreams.
How many days counts as success?
There's no fixed answer. 30 and 90 days are the numbers most often shared. This counter keeps your total and best alongside the streak, so slips still add up.
Where is my data stored?
Only in your device's browser. Nothing is sent to a server. To use another device, export the JSON and import it there.
What if I slip?
Press “Slipped” and pick the trigger. The streak goes back to 0, but your total and best record remain.
What does “No orgasm” mean?
Edging is allowed; the day counts as long as you don't finish. When an urge hits, the edging game is there to take it.

This page is a tool for logging abstinence days, not medical advice or treatment. If you are worried about your health, talk to a doctor. Your log is stored only on your device and never sent to a server. For adults 18+.
