How to play

How it works

  1. 01

    Press Start

    After 3, 2, 1 the notes begin. The screen stays awake while you play.

  2. 02

    One touch per note

    Eight rising notes (do-re-mi up to high do) are one shuttle out, then eight falling notes are one shuttle back. They alternate, and each finished shuttle adds one to your count. With a wand or vibrator, just hold it on you while the notes rise.

  3. 03

    Hands off on the way back

    In "With stops", the screen turns red on the way back. Take your fingers or toy away (if they're inside, just hold still and switch off any vibration) and wait until the notes start rising again.

  4. 04

    Tap "I came" twice when you come

    Two taps in a row end the run, and the shuttles you completed become your record. Level 21 ends at 247.

Tips

How to last longer

  • Keep it light for the first three levels

    It starts at about 60 notes a minute. Press hard here and you won't see the later levels.

  • Let go completely on the way back

    Keeping your fingers or toy there isn't a break. While it's red, pull away and breathe out.

  • Before it starts to sting

    More shuttles means more friction. If you get dry, use lube or spit. Stop if it starts to sting.

FAQ

FAQ

What is the Edging Beep Test?
The beep test is the school fitness test where you run back and forth between two lines, reaching the other side before each beep, and the beeps get faster every minute (the PACER test in the US, the bleep test in the UK). Here the notes speed up the same way, and you move along with them: one stroke or touch per note, faster about every minute. One shuttle is one PACER lap.
What's a good count?
The full run is 247 shuttles (level 21). Reaching level 8 at 62 shuttles is a good first target, and past 100 (level 11) you are lasting a long time.
What happens at the end?
Level 21 ends at shuttle 247. Start to finish takes about 22 minutes.
I can't hear anything
Sound starts after you press Start. On iPhone, turn off Silent mode or connect earphones. On Android, turn up the media volume.
Where is my record stored?
Only on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. A shared card shows just your count and level.
