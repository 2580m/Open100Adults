Tips

  • Plan the hour before bed

    “Bed” is the most common trigger. Decide where your phone goes before you lie down.

  • Use the tags

    Pick a tag when you slip. The stats show which situation catches you most often.

  • Watch the total, not only the streak

    A break resets the streak but not your total or best. Log it and continue.

    Most people say a strong urge fades within minutes. Use the 90-second button to ride it out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q.Does a wet dream count as a slip?

A.No. The counter only tracks things you decide to do. You don't need to log a wet dream.

Q.Is the day-7 testosterone claim true?

A.The 2003 study behind it was retracted in 2021. A small 10-person study found higher testosterone after three weeks, but there is no reliable evidence for day 7 specifically.

Q.Where is my log stored?

A.Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Export JSON to move it.

Q.What happens after a slip?

A.Press “I slipped”, pick a trigger, and the current streak restarts. Your total and best record stay.
