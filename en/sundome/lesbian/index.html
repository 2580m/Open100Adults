How to play

  1. 01

    Pick a duration, a difficulty and an ending

    Choose 3–30 minutes or set your own. Difficulty changes how often you must keep your hands off and your final permission chance. The ending decides what happens when the timer stops.

  2. 02

    Follow the colour and the beat

    Green means stroke. Yellow means slow and teasing. Red means hands off until it ends. On green and yellow the click keeps the beat: one stroke per beat.

  3. 03

    Report your edges

    When you manage to stop right before coming, press “Edged”. The game switches to a cooldown and your final chance rises by 5%, up to +20%.

  4. 04

    Hold out until the timer ends

    Coming early means you lose. If you're about to, let go completely and wait until it settles.

  5. 05

    The ending

    When time is up you either get permission or you're denied. Permission lasts 10 seconds. If you chose “Ruined”, let go the moment it starts.
