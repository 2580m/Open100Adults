Edging Beep Test

An edging test that gets harder as it goes.

One stroke per note of the rising scale. Stroke while the notes rise, hands off while they fall. The notes get faster every minute, and it ends when you come. Your record is how many shuttles you lasted.

How it works

  1. 01

    Press Start

    After 3, 2, 1 the notes begin. The screen stays awake while you play.

  2. 02

    One stroke per note

    Eight rising notes are one shuttle out, then eight falling notes are one shuttle back. Each finished shuttle adds one to your count.

  3. 03

    Hands off on the way back

    In “With stops”, the screen turns red on the way back. Let go and wait until the notes start rising again.

  4. 04

    Tap “I came” twice when you come

    Two taps in a row end the run, and the shuttles you completed become your record.

How to last longer

  • Stay loose for the first three levels

    It starts at about 60 notes a minute. Grip hard here and you won't see the later levels.

  • Let go completely on the way back

    Resting your hand or toy there isn't a break. While it's red, let go and breathe out.

  • Before it gets sore

    More shuttles means more friction. Use lube if you get dry and stop if anything hurts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q.What is the Edging Beep Test?

A.It borrows the school beep test: notes speed up every level and you keep pace. Here you do one stroke or touch per note instead of running.

Q.What counts as a shuttle?

A.One leg of eight notes. The rising leg is out, the falling leg is back. Each completed leg adds one.

Q.What does “With stops” change?

A.On the falling leg the screen turns red and you let go completely until the notes rise again. Continuous mode keeps stroking both ways.
