How to play

  1. 01

    Hit Start and play

    Roll the die, move along the board and do what the square says. Change edition or board length whenever you like. Your settings stay on this device.

  2. 02

    Roll, then do what the square says

    Climb from square 1 to the top, row by row. You can see the whole board before you roll.

  3. 03

    Ladders, snakes and Back to Start

    Land at the foot of a ladder and take its challenge to climb it. Land on a snake's head, do a short command, and slide down to its tail. The big Back to Start snake lets you stay only if you hold one edge.

  4. 04

    Land exactly on the Finish

    Roll too high and you bounce back from the Finish. After 3 bounces, any roll gets you there.

  5. 05

    Fail a command and slide back

    Can't do a command or break a rule, and you slide back 2 spaces (3 on a Tough square). It counts as a fail. Fewer fails means a better verdict.

The squares

Each square has a type. The board is visible before you roll, so you can plan around ladders and snakes.

  • TaskDo one command
  • EdgeGet right to the edge, then stop. Can't? Slide back 2
  • RestHands off. Calm down
  • ToughA tough command. Can't do it? Slide back 3
  • RuleA rule for your next 3 rolls
  • SnakeDo a short command, then slide down to its tail
  • LadderTake its challenge to climb it. Pass and you stay
  • TemptationOne extra, harder edge. Hold it and climb 2
  • CardDraw a Card. It tells you what happens
  • Back to StartHold one edge to stay. Can't? All the way back to Start
